# Tales of the Brave

*The little simulator that could.*

Data-driven world simulation framework supporting wide variety of gameplay styles and world setups.

Current MVP scope: Baltic Sea naval trade.

## Development status

This project is under active development.
Early versions are primarily intended for testing.

### Current version - approaching MVP 0.3

MVP stage version plan:

| Version | Reached | Tagline | Features |
|:-----------:|:-------:|:-------:|:---------------------|
| 0.1 | ✅ | Move It Move It | Map, movement, locations |
| 0.2 | ✅ | Dynamic Economy 101 | Trade commodities, economy simulation, selling & buying |
| 0.3 | ⏳ | Repairs & Resupplies | Maintenance implementation for player transport |
| 0.4 | ❌ | Eventually... | In-game events system (storms, economic shifts, etc.) |
| 0.5 | ❌ | Information Propagation Station | Information system for propagating and updating world knowledge |
| 0.6 | ❌ | Saving Our Souls | Persistent gameplay, external world definitions |
| 0.7 | ❌ | Questing Feast | Event-driven quests |
| 0.8 | ❌ | Upgrading Upgrade | Upgrades for player transport |
| 0.9 | ❌ | Narrative Takeover | Narrator system for gameplay records and world knowledge |
| 1.0 | ❌ | Public Launch! | Not much new, just polish and elbow grease. Finalized graphics and sfx |

Target public version 1.0 will contain:
* Baltic Sea map with multiple ports and locations to be discovered.
* Maintaining and upgrading your ship, hiring crew, and trying to keep them happy.
* Fluctuating economy that responds to player actions and world events.
* Information spreads through the world at its own pace, creating realistic uncertainty of i.e. not knowing exact prices at remote locations.
* Gameplay events ranging from mundane cargo floating overboard to pirate attacks to seasonal storms.
* Sidequests for transporting passengers, escorting other ships, or helping towns overcome shortages.
* Player's actions create extensive Captain's Log, recording the successes and misfortunes of your voyages.

Latest build at [Tales of the Brave](https://gamingdark.github.io/games/tales-of-the-brave/)
